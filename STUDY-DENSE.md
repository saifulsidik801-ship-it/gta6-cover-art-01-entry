## What v1 is missing vs top 1–2 (comparison, from the 4 v1 screenshots)
- Title too small and too polite. In 01-cover.jpg "PALM FEVER" is only ~55% of
  the frame width and ~15% of its height, sitting in a clear band under the
  hero instead of sprawling across the panels. Top 1–2 titles are ~90% wide,
  ~30–35% tall, and COVER the lower third of the photos. In 04-portrait.jpg
  "HIGH TIDE HUSTLE" is wide but short — tall thin letters with lots of bare
  photo above and below — and in 03-square.jpg "NEON HARBOR" has no retro
  stripes at all, just a flat pink-purple gradient.
- Too much bare photo and bare margin. Top 2 fills every band — location chip,
  starburst, quote strip, palm-silhouette strip, gradient tagline band, black
  footer with catalogue number — while v1's bottom is a single credit line and
  its panels carry only a role tag each. v1 has no location chip ("CORAL
  CAUSEWAY · 7:15 PM"), no "HOT SUMMER '25"-style starburst near the middle,
  no palm-silhouette strip above the footer, no catalogue number, no corner
  mark, and no second-line detail under any role label (top 4's
  "ROOFTOP BAR · 8:05 PM · OWNS THE SKYLINE" pattern).
- One panel short and no slanted cuts. v1 tops out at 6 panels; top 3/4/7 run
  7, and top 7/9 get extra energy from slanted dividers. v1's grid is all
  straight rectangles, so the eye reads it as a photo grid, not a comic cover.
- Hero pop-out is good but could be stronger. v1's centre hero already breaks
  its frame with a white outline (best in 01-cover.jpg) — top 10 does the same
  move weakly, so a thicker, cleaner halo plus a drop shadow is a real edge,
  but in 03-square.jpg the hero is just a tall centre column with no pop-out
  at all, and in 04-portrait.jpg the hero panel is half-buried behind the title.
- Editor screenshot (02-editor.jpg) is clear and usable, but the preview inside
  it inherits all of the above: the title looks small inside the editor frame
  too, and the layout thumbnails show six near-identical grids. The v2 fix is
  therefore in the COVER RENDERING first (bigger title, 7th panel, slanted
  dividers, chips / starburst / palm strip / catalogue / corner mark, role
  sub-lines), with the editor gaining 8 layouts × 6 grades × 6 caption presets
  so the density survives every shuffle.

## v2 density checklist (what the rebuild must show on screen)
1. Title ≥85% frame width, ~30% frame height, striped gradient, thick dark
   outline + drop shadow, overlapping panels — never sitting in a clear band.
2. 7 panels in the default layout + slanted-divider layout option + stronger
   white-halo hero pop-out with shadow.
3. Location chip + starburst badge + palm-silhouette strip + catalogue number
   + corner mark + role sub-lines on the cover at all times.
4. 8 layouts × 6 grades × 6 caption presets; 8 fresh sample photos; OFL fonts;
   no Rockstar assets; offline; PNG 1080×1080 / 1080×1350 / 1920×1080 / 1080×1920.
